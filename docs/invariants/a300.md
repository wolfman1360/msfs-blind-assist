# iniBuilds A300-600 — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/a300.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [a300.md](../a300.md).
The text is verbatim from CLAUDE.md on `feat/inibuilds-a300` as of `8f6ad85e`, moved here when that branch took the lean CLAUDE.md (#265); a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's.

## A300-1

- A map-driven A300 row is written by firing the control's OWN B: event, `value (>B:AIRLINER_<Name>_Set)`, through `ExecuteCalculatorCodeUnique` (`A300WritePlan`) — never `SetLVar` and never a bare L:var write. A TWO-POSITION switch's Set event FLIPS it whatever value it is given (iniBuilds' own `_ON`/`_OFF` bindings flip too, measured 2026-10-03), so a toggle or command row is sent only when its current position is KNOWN and differs, refused aloud ("position unknown, try again in a moment") otherwise, and a just-commanded position is trusted for 2.5 s over the 1 Hz cache (`A300CommandedState`); a flip sent the wrong way inverts the switch. Selectors and knobs are absolute; hold buttons are Set 2, 250 ms, Set 0, except the six fire tests (`A300WritePlan.TestHoldMs`: the aircraft's own checklist's 5 s for the APU loop test and 3 s for the squib tests, 250 ms never finished one, measured 2026-10-04; 7 s for the engine loop tests, whose fire warning comes at about 4.4 s and lasts while held, so 5 s lit the handle only half a second, measured 2026-10-09), and the takeoff config, landing gear warning and smoke tests, held 5 s (a 250 ms takeoff config press reported nothing even in landing configuration); spring switches are held 1 s and returned to rest. The flaps lever, ground spoiler arm and speed brake are the hand-written exception (`A300Levers`: `K:FLAPS_SET` index x 4096, `K:SPOILERS_ARM_SET` read back from `L:INI_SPOILERS_ARMED` because the stock armed flag stays 0, `K:SPOILERS_SET`). Every write is refused aloud ("{name} unavailable") when `CalcWriteCanLand` is false and the row is asked for again so its combo snaps back. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-2

- `MSFSBlindAssist/Resources/a300_control_map.json` is GENERATED by `tools/a300-gen` from the aircraft's compiled cockpit behaviour and tooltips — never hand-edit it; regenerate after an iniBuilds update and review with `diff_maps.py`. iniBuilds' files and any decoded copy of them stay out of the repo. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-3

- `A300PanelLayout` is the ONLY source of A300 panels: WHICH panel a control is on follows iniBuilds' own component tree (so an update is picked up by regenerating), except where `ControlPanels` (`A300PanelLayout.Systems.cs`) sends a control of a place-grouped iniBuilds panel to its system's panel, and `PanelSections` puts that panel in its fleet section. Owner decision, 2026-10-09: the fleet's Airbuses group by system, so the overhead lights panel is Signs, Interior Lighting and Exterior Lighting, the bleed panel is Bleed and Air Conditioning, Cargo Smoke leaves Fire, "Main Panel" is the FBW "Instrument" section (Warnings, Gear, Autobrake, Thrust Rating Panel, Landing Elevation, Standby Instruments, Source Switching, Clock, GPWS, Captain Side, First Officer Side) and the throttle quadrant is Engines, Thrust Levers (with the go levers, which iniBuilds puts on the captain's EFIS panel), Flaps and Speed Brake and Parking Brake, its cancel buttons and takeoff config test joining the ECAM Control Panel; the fleet's names (Pressurization, EFIS Captain, Transponder, Audio Control Panel Captain, Recorder) and the manual's where the A300 differs (IRS, Flight Controls, SAS Control, Thrust Rating Panel). Window and probe heat and the landing elevation knob stay where the A300 has them (owner, same day). Rows both sides share a panel with say their side first ("Captain chrono"). The ORDER is `A300PanelLayout.Order.cs`: `PanelOrder` is the FBW A320's order where the A300 has the same panel, and `LeadRows` opens panels on what the A300 checklist reaches first; everything else keeps cockpit-file order, and a panel or row the lists miss stays after the listed ones, never dropped. It also splits the pedestal's "STD" radio and audio group by what each control is, names every unclear or clashing control in `A300PanelLayout.Names.cs` (iniBuilds' titles repeat, and two are wrong: "ALTITUDE KNOB" on the V/S knob, "CPT BAROMETER" on the first officer's), and puts a guard directly before its switch. Guard COVERS are never rows (they only move the model); the two cargo smoke agent guards are real switches the agent switch depends on. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-4

- The A300 MCDU screens come from the client data areas `iniAirbusMCDU_1`/`_2` (1008 bytes), published only while `L:INI_MCDU_OPTION` is 1 — which does NOT survive a flight load, so the MCDU window writes it on EVERY open. `A300McduDataManager` keeps the MD-11 manager's rules: one per connection (reused, never re-registered, on the same handle), ON_SET + CHANGED subscription PLUS a one-time snapshot on a DIFFERENT request id. Read characters through `A300McduText.Glyph` — the MCDU fonts draw `!` as an entry box, `^` as a degree sign and `a`/`b`/`l`/`r`/`{`/`}`/`~` as arrows and a delta (decoded from the glyph outlines), never as the letters they are. MCDU keys are `INI_MCDU1_`/`INI_MCDU2_` L:var pulses through the definition's ONE queue: a press and release in ONE calculator string does not register. Never press CLR on an empty scratchpad (the A300 puts "CLR" into it); the clear-all re-reads after each press. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-5

- A300 typed values go through `A300TypedValues` only. NAV frequencies are written as their `_MHZ`/`_KHZ` L:var parts (the combined `INI_VOR1_FREQUENCY` and the stock NAV radio are rebuilt from them, so writing either is silently undone). A typed speed in the other unit sends SPD/MACH, WAITS `ModeSwitchSettleMs`, then writes — never in one string: the aircraft applies the switch later and converts whatever the variable holds then. Altimeters are `index value (>K:2:KOHLSMAN_SET)`, index FIRST. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-6

- The A300 tablet reader (`coherent-a300-efb-agent.js`) presses with ONE click event (pointer and mouse down/up, then click; a bare `el.click()` is ignored and two clicks would run a toggle twice), never requires a non-zero size on a CONTAINER (the page container is 2048 x 0), never `focus()`es a field (the on-screen keyboard covers the page), and never reads a password field's value. The press goes where a finger on the control's centre would land (`A.pressTarget`: the topmost element there when it is inside the control, else the first inner element with its own onclick), NEVER on the stamped control alone: the tablet hangs the Unlock and gear handlers on the picture INSIDE the button, so a press on the button did nothing and the tablet could not be unlocked (2026-10-04). An overlay (control box, its confirmation, powered off, pause, My Flight's viewers) is read ALONE under its own page name, and found with `A.shown` (every ancestor displayed), never `A.visible`, which looks at one element and lets a hidden page's viewer pass. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-7

- The master warning and master caution speak only as they come ON: pressing the light writes 0 to the same `INI_MASTER_*_ACTIVE` variable, so speaking it going out would announce the pilot's own press. Lamps and the four announced levers speak from `ProcessSimVarUpdate` inside MainForm's mute and echo wrap (`DefAnnounceMuteSets` lists `INI_A300`); a context reset wipes the baselines and `Md11SeedGate` decides when the unchanged ones are seeded from the cache. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-8

- The A300 display readers read ONLY aircraft variables and stock SimVars: never the simulator's memory, a picture or AI (the owner's rule, 2026-10-04). So the upper ECAM's memos and warnings and the ND's waypoint name and bearing are NOT read: they live inside the aircraft's display software, not in any variable. Do not "complete" them with a memory read or an AI capture without asking the owner again. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-9

- The FMA (`A300Fma`) transcribes the A300's own PFD::drawFMA (v1.0.11) word table, characterised by running that routine on chosen mode numbers; change a word only from a new characterisation, never by analogy with the A320. It is composed once a sample at the end of the batch that carries its sources (`DeferredFlushWatchVariable` = the pitch mode key, all 27 sources in ONE batch, pinned by `IniA300FmaBehaviourTests`; the batch boundary is at the 300th name in ordinal order, so ANY new batch-covered A300 variable can push the last sources, `INI_pitch_trim1/2`, into batch 2 — the 58 fault lights did, which is why they have their own subscriptions), and spoken there, OUTSIDE MainForm's mute wrap, so it checks `A300DisabledMonitorVariables` itself (`IsMuted`). Six sources are shared rows and lights (the autothrottle and SPD/MACH lights, both A/THR master switches, both autopilot switches, both pitch trim switches) and must never be registered a second time. The thrust column is blanked together with the guidance columns while the PFD would draw none (an IRS aligned, essential bus 2 powered, pitch trim 2 or pitch trim 1 with the captain's FD source): a DELIBERATE divergence, because the mode numbers keep old values cold and dark. Baseline-first; the guidance columns appearing and a settling flight load (`Md11SeedGate` armed) are silent. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-10

- The A300 display boxes read: green dot, S and F only at the flap lever positions the speed tape draws them (0; 1; 2 and 3 — `PFD::drawSpeedTape`); VMAX is `INI_max_speed` (the tape's top, 270 with the gear down), not `INI_VMAX_SPEED` (VMO); the standby altimeter is `INDICATED ALTITUDE:3` (it follows baro 3, measured); the ILS receiver is NAV 3; the fuel tank weights are KILOGRAMS (they sum to the stock total) and fuel reads in the tablet's unit (`L:INI_IS_METRIC`). An ECAM page lists only what its own drawing routine PRINTS as a number; a needle with no printed number or a value whose unit the page does not establish is left out, never guessed. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-11

- The A300 take-off call-outs (`A300TakeoffCallouts`) take V1 and VR from `L:INI_V1_FMGS`/`L:INI_VR_FMGS` (the MCDU take-off page) and V2 from `L:INI_V2_FMGS`, which FOLLOWS THE FCU SPEED WINDOW (250 with V2 typed as 150, measured 2026-10-04), because the A300 crew sets V2 on the FCU for take-off. So V2 is used only when `PlausibleV2` accepts it: at or above VR and at most 30 knots above. Never read `INI_V2_FMGS` as the MCDU's V2, and never announce its changes (every FCU speed change moves it). The arm is dropped on every context reset AND reconnect, never the speeds (the shared machine's rule). → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-12

- The A300 fault lights (`A300FaultLights`, 55; 58 until the fire handle lights moved to `A300PanelLamps`, 2026-10-09) stream on their OWN once-a-second subscriptions (`ExcludeFromBatch`), never the continuous batch, to keep the FMA's sources in one batch. Each change is collected in `ProcessSimVarUpdate` only when `announcer.Suppressed` is false THERE (MainForm's mute and echo wrap) and spoken at the next continuous batch end as ONE sentence (`A300LampCallouts`), so a muted light must never be collected and spoken later outside the wrap. Names come from mapping the cockpit model's emissive nodes to the light variables; never name a light from its variable alone (`INI_ENGINE1_BLEED_LIGHT` lights a wing anti-ice node and is left out for that reason). → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-13

- A300 autopilot and autothrottle engagement call-outs (`A300EngagementTracker`) are silent for the pilot's own MSFSBA pick, judged by `A300CommandedState` (the row or hotkey recorded the commanded position within 2.5 s); never by a value compare against the cache alone, which cannot tell a yoke button from the panel. Their Ctrl+M rows ride `INI_AUTOPILOT_DISCONNECT` and `INI_AUTOTHROTTLE_DISCONNECTED`, registered only to carry the rows; nothing reads their values, which were never measured. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-14

- Of the A300 FCU windows, ONLY the altitude window is announced on its own (`A300FcuWindows`): `INI_Altitude_Dial` is written by the knob handlers alone, while the aircraft's own autopilot logic also writes the heading, speed and vertical speed windows (heading sync, managed speed, `SyncVerticalSpeed`), so announcing those would speak changes nobody made. A panel knob step on any of the four is read back once it lands; MSFSBA's own typed altitude or altitude step suppresses the altitude call-out for 2.5 s. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-15

- The A300 decision height is `L:INI_MINIMUMS_PILOT` (and `_FO`): the stored value, written directly by the typed Decision height box. The DH knob changes it only while `L:INI_DH_SELECTED` is 1 and steps the flight path angle otherwise, so a knob-based setter would silently move the FPA instead. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-16

- Measured live 2026-10-05 at the gate (external power and batteries on, package 1.0.11, through the Coherent debugger on VCockpit01 with the same B: events the app fires). Pulling the captain's or first officer's altimeter knob is STD and pushing is QNH, as iniBuilds' tooltips say ("SET STD PRESSURE", "SET QNH PRESSURE"); the push event writes a variable named `INI_<side>_ALTIMETER_STD_COMMAND`, so never trust the name. `L:XMLVAR_Baro1_Mode` / `Baro2_Mode` read 1 in STD and 0 in QNH and drive the altimeter's STD flag; the standby (Baro3) has no push or pull. A pull saves the setting × 16 in `L:INI_BARO1_PRESSURE` / `INI_BARO2_PRESSURE`. But STD is a FLAG ONLY: the Kohlsman setting does not change, the drums keep reading against it (500 feet at 1024.94 in STD against a pressure altitude of 197), the knob still turns in STD, a push does not restore the saved value, and `A:KOHLSMAN SETTING STD` stays 0. So Ctrl+B's STD pulls only the sides in QNH (pulling a side already in STD would save 1013 over its QNH) and then writes 1013.25 to all three altimeters; QNH pushes the sides in STD and restores each one's saved setting, the standby taking the captain's. A side already in QNH is never restored (what it saved may be older than what it shows), and a saved setting outside 955 to 1060 hectopascals is reported, never used. Each press is confirmed by reading the mode back before anything is written. The mode variables stream on their own subscriptions, never the batch ([A300-9]). → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-17

- `BaseAircraftDefinition.ShowTrackedWindow` keys its registry on the window's TYPE, and all five A300 value boxes (Ctrl+S, H, A, V, B) are `ValueInputForm`, which stays open after Set. Without a check, a second hotkey found the open box under the same type and re-showed it: with the speed box open, Ctrl+H brought back the speed box. `ShowValueDialog` therefore closes an open box whose title differs before asking for the new one (`A300AutoflightWindows.ReplacesOpenBox`); closing removes it from the registry, so the factory builds the requested box. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-18

- The input-mode windows name a control exactly as its panel row does (`A300PanelLayout`), so one control has one name across the app, and add what a knob push or pull does in iniBuilds' own tooltip words, which the generator copies into the map's `action` field ("Heading knob push, aircraft heading"). Words that only repeat the name are dropped ("SET SPEED KNOB" on the speed knob pull). Each control sits in one window only: the value boxes carry their axis's knob push and pull and mode buttons, and Ctrl+P carries the engage cluster, the approach modes (VOR LOC, Land), the disconnects, TOGA, the bank limit and the flight directors, as on the PMDG. Every press goes through the panel row's write path, so the refusals ("unavailable", "position unknown") and the commanded-state hold match the panels. In the value boxes a lamp button is read back fresh and a knob push or pull says nothing; Ctrl+P says nothing on a press. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-20

- The LO, MED and MAX autobrake buttons show what their lamps show, read from the aircraft's own code (1.0.11): each button's lower segment lights while `L:INI_AUTOBRAKE_LEVEL` equals its level (1, 2, 3; 0 is off) and its DECEL segment is dark, and the DECEL segment lights from `L:INI_AUTOBRAKE_LOW_DECEL` / `MED_DECEL` / `HI_DECEL` (the cockpit's emissive code), which `update_ab_datarefs` sets only at that level, engaged, past 1.36 of deceleration. So the label reads "Armed", "Decel" or "Off" (`A300Autobrake.Describe`), and nothing while the level or the AC light power is unread. `L:INI_autobrake_armed` is never the state: only the three button handlers (`INI_autobrake_low_handler` and its siblings) write it, while the aircraft's own disarms in `INI_autobrake` (the gear handle leaving down, its disconnects) set the level to 0 and leave it at 1. A press writes `INI_ABRK_LOW/MED/HI_COMMAND`: the active level pressed again disarms by the aircraft's design (flight 2, measured), so it is never blocked and is silent; any other level arms only with the gear handle down, green hydraulic pressure at 2,200 psi or more, no autobrake fault and, with the parking brake off, `INI_brake_system` at 0. A press says nothing; the lights are spoken as they change, as the Fenix speaks its autobrake lights, through the same `A300LampBoard` as the fault lights ([A300-23]): both segments are on AC light power, so a label also reads "Off" on a dark AC bus. A press the aircraft refuses changes no light, so nothing is said (the owner dropped a "did not arm" read-back on 2026-10-09: no other aircraft says it). The level and the three DECEL lights stream on their own subscriptions, never the batch ([A300-9]); the level's Ctrl+M row ("Autobrake armed lights") mutes the three armed lights, and each DECEL light has its own row. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-21

- The thrust rating panel, read from the aircraft's own code (1.0.11). A TRP button's Set writes `L:INI_TRP_<X>_BUTTON` 1, which `TRP::Update` consumes (measured on flight 1: a write of 1 is consumed): TOGA, MCT, CL, CR, AUTO and FLEX TO set `L:INI_TRP_MODE` to 1 to 6, and each button's lamp is `INI_TRP_MODE` == its number (the cockpit's emissive code), so the label reads "On" only for that mode (`A300Trp.ButtonState`). In AUTO the mode STAYS 5 (the AUTO handler writes 5, and `TRP::Update` writes 5 itself in a profile climb past the thrust reduction altitude), while `L:INI_TRP_AUTO_MODE` holds the limit the aircraft chose by flight phase, 1 TOGA, 3 climb, 4 cruise (`updateN1limit`; flight 1 measured 1 on the ground and 3 in the climb): reading the limit as a lit button would say "climb: On" while the cockpit lights AUTO. The autopilot logic can also write 1 and the MCDU's MODE page 2. `L:INI_THRUST_MAXIMUM_N1` is the active limit's N1 (`updateN1limit` copies the mode's own N1 into it), but with PW engines (`L:INI_IS_PW` 1) the TRP shows EPR limits, so the N1 limit is read only while `INI_IS_PW` reads 0. `L:INI_FLEX_TEMPERATURE` is the FMS's flex temperature, copied there on every update (`FMGS::Update`), so a direct write is undone; the knob steps it one degree, 0 to 72, and each step is read back once it lands, as the FCU knobs' are ([A300-14]). The Center Panel's status box has one "TRP" line (`A300Trp.Line`: "AUTO, climb, N1 limit 95.3 percent", "FLEX, 45 degrees"). The mode, AUTO's limit, the N1 limit and the engine type stream on their own subscriptions, consumed silently, never the batch ([A300-9]). → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-22

- Ctrl+B (owner-approved design, 2026-10-09). The box has ONE mode button, labelled from the modes the aircraft REPORTS (`XMLVAR_Baro1/2_Mode`, [A300-16]), never the one just asked for: "Altimeter mode: QNH" or "STD" while both sides agree, "captain STD, first officer QNH" while they differ, and "Altimeter mode" alone while neither has been read (`A300Baro.ModeState`), as the FBW A320 window's Mode control reads its mode. A press runs the STD sequence only when both sides read QNH, and the QNH sequence otherwise, so sides that differ both end in QNH (the side already in QNH is left alone; the FBW A320's control reads one side only, so it has no rule of its own for this: the owner's default); a side unread is refused aloud (`A300Baro.ToggleFor`). Each sequence speaks its own settings read-back, so the box's echo is off. A TYPED value with a side in STD first pushes every side in STD (the standby has none) and confirms each reads QNH by reading its mode back (`PressKnobsAsync`); a side that stays in STD is named ("First officer altimeter did not switch to QNH") and nothing is written, because on flight 2 (2026-10-06, descending through FL180) a value typed with both sides in STD reached only the standby, the captain and first officer staying at 29.92 with no word said. Then the value goes to all three altimeters and is confirmed as before. An unread mode is refused aloud, and so is a typed value while a sequence is running (its decision would come from the 1 Hz cache, which still shows the old modes). The PMDG 737's typed altimeter takes STD off first the same way (`DisengageStdThenRotateAsync`). The value field is named with both units (`A300Baro.FieldName`: "altimeter setting in inches or hectopascals"), through the field name the A300 passes the shared `ValueInputForm`. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-23

- Every A300 cockpit lamp, in the aircraft's emissive code (1.0.11, `A300_Interior.behavior.xml`), is `(state OR INI_ANNLT_SWITCH == 0) × INI_GENERAL_LIGHT_MULTIPLIER × its bus's light power`. The power flags are written by one block: `INI_DC_LIGHTS_FAILURE` = battery on OR the AC emergency bus powered, `INI_AC_LIGHTS_FAILURE` = the AC essential bus powered (1 = powered, despite the name); the multiplier is 1 (ANN LT DIM) or 10, never 0, so it is not a power term. Each `A300FaultLights` entry carries its bus from its own expression (26 AC, 29 DC; the three fire handle lights have no power term), and the autobrake segments are on AC (armed: level == n AND its DECEL dark; DECEL: its variable). ONE `A300LampBoard` decides the fault and autobrake lights: lit = state AND power; a light's first value with every input read is a silent baseline, and an unread input leaves it undecided, so a cold-and-dark start or a power change never reads lights the cockpit does not show. Measured 2026-10-09 at the gate: switching the aircraft off read "2 lights on: Cabin regulator 2 fault, Battery 1 fault" and more as systems lost power while the panel went dark; switching on read lights "off" that were never heard on. With the board, power coming on speaks the lights that light, power going off speaks the lit ones going out, and a fault set or cleared on a dark bus says nothing. A power flag, the autobrake level or a DECEL light changes several lights at once, so those keys are `IsMuteWrapExempt` and each change checks its own light's Ctrl+M row (`IsMuted(MuteKey)`, VAR-8); the status box of a fault light reads On only while lit (`TryDescribeControlState`, repainted on its power key through `StateVariables`). The annunciator test term is left out, as before: the test says nothing. The two flags stream on their own subscriptions, consumed silently, no Ctrl+M row, never the batch ([A300-9]). → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-24

- The light power flags (`INI_AC/DC_LIGHTS_FAILURE`) and the fault flags each reach MSFSBA on their own once-a-second subscriptions ([A300-12], [A300-23]), so the order the aircraft set them in is lost. Measured on external power at PANC, 2026-10-09, sampling the cockpit every 250 ms: the rudder travel limiter 2 and cabin regulator 2 faults cleared at 1043 ms and the AC light power rose at 1548 ms, but MSFSBA heard the power first and said "10 lights on: ... Rudder travel limiter system 2 fault, Cabin regulator 2 fault, ... ECAM clear", then within a second "Rudder travel limiter system 2 fault light off", "2 lights off: Cabin regulator 2 fault, Cockpit door fault", "ECAM clear light off". External power off had the same pair inside ONE sentence ("Cabin regulator 2 fault light on. 9 lights off: ... Cabin regulator 2 fault").
- `A300LampSpeech` takes every board change the definition keeps (after the mute checks), and `FlushLamps` asks it for a sentence at each continuous batch end. It nets per light: a light that changed an even number of times since the last sentence ends where it started and says nothing; otherwise its last change stands, in the order of the last changes. When `A300LampBoard.PowerFlips` says a delivery turns a bus's light power on or off (a first value is a baseline, never a flip), the definition calls `NotePowerChange`, and nothing is spoken until `SettleMs` (2500 ms: two subscription periods and a batch end) has passed; the changes gathered meanwhile are netted and spoken as one sentence. The flags arrive in either order: on external power off (2026-10-09) the rudder travel limiter 2 fault reached MSFSBA a second BEFORE the AC light power flag, and was said on, then, in the settled sentence, off. So the first change since the last sentence also waits `GatherMs` (1500 ms, one subscription period and a little), which lets a power flag still on its way extend the hold and net with it; a light is spoken about 1.5 s after it changes (the MD-11 waits 1.5 s before speaking a lamp going dark). A context reset clears both the pending changes and the hold.
- This is the fleet's behaviour, settled: the Fenix speaks every lamp a power change lights or darkens (its lamp variables already include power, so it needs no settling; measured 30 to 55 announcements per power step), and the MD-11, which composes its lamps with a DC gate as the A300 does, also waits before speaking a lamp going dark (`DarkSettleMs`). The owner chose this on 2026-10-09 over a silent power-up.
- Live comparison, same four steps (external power off, batteries off, batteries on, external power on), 2026-10-09: 13 announcements before, 9 after, no on-then-off pairs; a power change's sentence comes about 4 to 5 s after the press instead of about 2 s. Lights changing with power steady are unaffected. → [a300.md](../a300.md)

## A300-25

- Most of the cockpit's lamps are not one variable times its bus's light power. External power AVAIL is "ground power available AND external power not on", with no power term (the ground power unit lights it); the gear greens are the stock `GEAR POSITION:n` at 100 percent; many lamps light while a variable is clear. So `tools/a300-gen` copies every annunciator lamp's own state rule from its emissive code into `a300_control_map.json` (`lamps`: node, state in the cockpit's RPN, bus), 496 of them, leaving out the test-only lamps (a bare number, or only the test switch, as iniBuilds' SELCAL lamps) and panel backlights (no test term). A Material's OwnerID is the index of its Component in document order, the root component included (an index that skips the root names the wrong node: the battery's OFF legend came out as battery 2's).
- `A300LampRule` evaluates a rule over what MSFSBA reads: variables, stock variables in their unit, numbers, and only the operators the lamps use (`! and or == != > < /`); anything else is refused, and `A300LampRuleTests` parses every shipped lamp. `A300LampBoard` decides each named lamp as rule AND power, through the same netting and settling as every light ([A300-24]).
- `A300PanelLamps` names the lamps a pilot reads, panel by panel as each is swept (owner decision 2026-10-09: every light that can be read is spoken): the button, its legend, "light" ([VAR-1]).
