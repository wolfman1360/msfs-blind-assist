READ THIS FIRST - WHAT THIS VERSION COVERS

Choose iniBuilds L-1011 TriStar from the Aircraft menu. This version covers the TriStar's
switch panels: the flight engineer's station, the overhead, the main instrument panel and the
center console, in the order the TriStar's own preflight flows call for them. Not included yet:
the glareshield autopilot panel, the three INS units and the two PMS units, and the Paper Flight
Bag tablet. Until they arrive, operate those in the simulator.


THE PANELS

Sections (Ctrl+1): Engineer Station, Overhead, Main Panel, Center Console. The panels (Ctrl+2)
follow the preflight flows: the Engineer Station starts with Circuit Breakers, lights, passenger
oxygen and Electrical; the Overhead with the INS mode selectors and the engine fire handles.

A switch is a combo box with the aircraft's own position words. A push button is a button. A
dimmer or temperature knob is a slider. A knob that only turns, such as a pressurization rate
knob, is a pair of buttons, "increase" and "decrease". A guard comes just before the switch it
covers.

When you change a control, MSFS Blind Assist moves it exactly the way a click in the cockpit
would. Nothing is spoken back - your screen reader already says what you chose - unless the
control could not be moved, when you hear why: "Battery unavailable" (the simulator connection
cannot carry the change), or "position unknown, try again in a moment" (a few switches must be
read before they may be moved; this clears within a second or two of connecting).

Each panel's gauges and warning lights are in its Status Display (Ctrl+3): fuel per tank,
hydraulic pressures, cabin altitude, engine EPR, N1, N2, temperatures and fuel flow, the radios,
and the lights on that panel.


TYPED VALUES - type, then press Set or Enter

  Altimeters (Main Panel section: Captain, First Officer and Standby Instruments) - type inches
  (29.92) or hectopascals (1013). You hear "Captain altimeter 1013, 29.92".
  NAV 1 and NAV 2 (Center Console, Navigation Radios) - megahertz, 108.00 to 117.95.
  COM 1, 2 and 3, active and standby (Center Console, VHF Radios) - megahertz, 118.000 to
  136.990. Setting an active frequency puts it in standby and swaps it in.
  Squawk (Center Console, Transponder) - four digits, each 0 to 7.


CIRCUIT BREAKERS

The Engineer Station's Circuit Breakers panel has an "Open circuit breaker list" button. The
window lists all 981 breakers ("HYD IND QTY: in"); the ones iniBuilds left unnamed read
"Breaker 12: in". Type in Search to narrow the list, tick "Show pulled breakers only" to see
what is pulled, and press Space or Enter on a breaker to pull it or push it in. The row changes
to the new state. The states are read when the window opens; Refresh reads them again.

BACKGROUND ANNOUNCEMENTS - Ctrl+M

Warning and caution lights are announced when they come on and when they go out ("Engine 1 fire
light on"), once they have stayed that way for about a second, so a flashing light is heard
once. The annunciator light test, which lights every lamp at once, is silent.

The flap handle, the parking brake and the ground spoiler arm are announced when something
other than MSFS Blind Assist moves them - a hardware lever, or the aircraft's own automation
("Flaps 14 degrees", "Parking brake set"). Changes you make in MSFS Blind Assist are not.

Ctrl+M opens the monitor manager: type in the search box to narrow the list, use the Show filter
to see only the muted rows, and untick an item to stop it announcing. Your choices are
remembered between sessions.
