READ THIS FIRST - WHAT THIS VERSION COVERS

Choose iniBuilds Airbus A300-600 from the Aircraft menu. The passenger and freighter versions,
with either engine, share one cockpit, so one choice covers all four. This version covers the
A300's switch panels: the overhead, the glareshield, the main instrument panel, the pedestal
and the cockpit's back wall, both MCDUs as text (Shift+M), and the FCU, autopilot and radios with
typed values. Not included yet: the tablet, and readouts of the PFD, ND and ECAM screens (so the
flight mode annunciator cannot be read yet). Until they arrive, operate those in the simulator.
Shift+T says so instead of opening a window.


THE PANELS

Sections (Ctrl+1): Overhead, Glareshield, Main Panel, Pedestal, Cockpit, Cargo. The panels
(Ctrl+2) follow the cockpit as iniBuilds built it: the Overhead starts with Cabin Pressure, IRS,
APU and Anti-Ice; the Glareshield holds the FCU and both EFIS panels; the Pedestal holds the
throttle quadrant, the radios, the transponder, the weather radar and the ECAM control panel.
The Cargo section is the freighter's main deck cargo door panel.

A switch is a combo box with the aircraft's own position words. A push button is a button. A
dimmer or temperature knob is a slider. A knob that only turns, such as the FCU speed knob or a
radio frequency knob, is a pair of buttons, "increase" and "decrease", each one click of the
knob. The flaps lever, the ground spoiler arm and the speed brake lever are at the end of the
Throttle Quadrant panel.

When you change a control, MSFS Blind Assist moves it exactly the way a click in the cockpit
would. Nothing is spoken back - your screen reader already says what you chose - unless the
control could not be moved, when you hear why: "Battery 1 unavailable" (the simulator connection
cannot carry the change), or "position unknown, try again in a moment" (a switch must be read
before it may be moved; this clears within a second or two of connecting).

A trim switch (aileron trim, rudder trim, the pitch trim switches on the yokes, the cabin
manual vertical speed switch) springs back: choosing a side holds it there for one second and
lets it return, the way a short press does in the cockpit. Choose the side again for more.

Each cargo smoke agent switch has its guard just before it. The switch only moves while its
guard is open, exactly as in the aircraft.

Status Display (Ctrl+3): the FCU panel shows the four FCU windows; the Captain, First Officer
and Center panels show the altimeter settings, the flex temperature and the landing elevation;
the Navigation Radios panel shows the VOR and ILS courses. The Captain Panel also shows the
master warning and master caution lights.


TYPED VALUES - type, then press Set or Enter

  FCU panel: Speed or Mach (100 to 399 knots, or Mach 0.10 to 0.99; a value in the other unit
  switches SPD/MACH for you), Heading (0 to 360), Altitude (100 to 49,000 feet, nearest 100),
  Vertical speed (-6,000 to 6,000, nearest 100). You hear "Heading 270".
  Navigation Radios panel: VOR 1 and VOR 2 frequency (108.00 to 117.95) and course setting, ILS
  frequency (108.10 to 111.95) and course setting.
  VHF Radios panel: VHF 1 and VHF 2 standby frequency (118.000 to 136.990); the transfer buttons
  swap them in.
  Transponder and TCAS panel: Squawk, four digits each 0 to 7.
  Captain, First Officer and Center panels: the altimeter settings, in inches (29.92) or
  hectopascals (1013).

The FCU's mode buttons say whether their mode is on: "Heading select: On", "Level change: Off".
SPD/MACH says "Speed" or "Mach". The FCU panel's status display shows the four windows.


THE MCDU WINDOW - Press left bracket, then Shift+M

Both MCDUs, Captain and First Officer, read as text: the real screens, live. The window opens on
the screen list: the title, then each line with its label above it, numbered 1 to 6 for the
line-select keys, then the scratchpad. When the page changes you hear its title and the cursor
goes to line 1. The window switches on the aircraft's MCDU export (the tablet's External
Hardware setting) every time it opens, because the aircraft forgets it at each flight load.

How the screen reads: # is an empty box the MCDU wants filled (dashes are optional fields), the
arrows beside a title mean there are more pages (Right Arrow or Alt+Right goes to the next one),
and a degree sign is a degree sign. A blank screen says so: the MCDU is probably unpowered.

  Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+6   Left line-select keys 1 to 6
  Alt+1 to Alt+6     Right line-select keys 1 to 6
                     (or F1 to F6 and F7 to F12, with the alternate layout in FMC Settings)
  Page Up / Down     Slew up and down
  Right Arrow        Next page (on the screen list); Alt+Right from anywhere in the window
  Backspace          CLR, one character (on the screen list)
  Delete             Clear the whole scratchpad (on the screen list)
  Alt+S              Go to the input box: type, then Enter sends it to the scratchpad
  Alt+Home           Back to the screen list
  Ctrl+Shift+L / R   Captain or First Officer MCDU
  Escape             Close the window
  Page keys          Alt+I Init, Alt+F F-Plan, Alt+P Prog, Alt+D Dir To, Alt+R Ref, Alt+O Mode,
                     Alt+T Tact Mode, Alt+A T/O Appr, Alt+E Eng Out, Alt+L Sec Plan, Alt+M Menu,
                     Alt+C Clr, Alt+N Next Page, Alt+U slew up, Alt+W slew down

The scratchpad is read back once what you typed has landed. An entry holding a character the MCDU
keyboard does not have (a comma, for example) is not sent at all, and stays in the box to edit.

BACKGROUND ANNOUNCEMENTS - Ctrl+M

The master warning and master caution lights are announced when they come on ("Master
warning"). Pressing the light to acknowledge it is not spoken back.

The flaps lever, the ground spoiler arm, the gear lever and the parking brake are announced when
something other than MSFS Blind Assist moves them - a hardware lever, or the aircraft itself
("Flaps 15/15", "Parking brake set"). Changes you make in MSFS Blind Assist are not.

Ctrl+M opens the monitor manager: type in the search box to narrow the list, use the Show filter
to see only the muted rows, and untick an item to stop it announcing. Your choices are
remembered between sessions.
